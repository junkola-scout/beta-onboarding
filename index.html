Google Workspace · One connection · Required

About two minutes.

Install for your team

What Junkola reads

  • Gmail: work email, to find requests and commitments
  • Calendar: who met, when, and about what
  • Meet: meeting transcripts
  • Drive: project documents and reports

What Junkola never does

  • Use your information to train AI
  • Send, change or delete anything in Google
  • Share your information outside your company
  • Keep it after you disconnect and ask us to delete

What happens next

You land on your board right away. Your own last 7 days appear first, usually within a minute. Your team's last 30 days fill in behind.
